Three rules
1

The last two reps should be a fight

Pick a weight where the last two reps of each set are really hard, close to failure, with your form still clean. If they feel easy, go heavier next time.

2

No gym? No problem

Every session has a home version. Busy day, travel, rain: switch to "Home" on the session page and train where you are.

3

Two lower days never back to back

Move your days around as you need, as long as Lower A and Lower B always have at least one day between them.

Food & supplements

On your plate

  • Real food. Nothing processed.
  • Added sugar: less is more. Keeping it low steadies your energy and keeps your blood sugar balanced. Your blood test suggests that's worth a little attention. Fruit like bananas is perfectly fine.
  • Swap fast carbs (white bread, pastries, juice, sweets) for slow ones: oats, legumes, potatoes, quinoa. Gluten-free alternatives work too.
  • Protein at every meal.
  • Alcohol as little as possible.
  • No coffee after 4 pm.

In your cupboard

  • Omega-3 (EPA/DHA): with a meal that contains some fat.
  • Vitamin C: in the morning, with breakfast.
  • Magnesium bisglycinate: before bed, for deeper sleep.
  • Vegan collagen capsules: for skin and recovery.
  • Sleep: 7 hours or more. It's when your muscles, your skin and your energy recover.

Creatine: yes, but ask your doctor first

You can try creatine, but check with your doctor first. Your blood test shows creatinine slightly above range and a kidney filter value (eGFR 66) that is mildly reduced. Creatine raises creatinine in your blood by itself, so once you take it, your doctor can no longer read your kidney values properly.

If everything looks fine: 3 g a day, no loading phase. Expect 0.5–1 kg more on the scale in the first weeks. That's water stored in your muscles, not fat.

4×4 intervals

Four blocks of four minutes where your heart works hard, with strength moves in between. It's the most time-efficient session for belly fat in your week, and the one that changes most often, so it never gets boring.
