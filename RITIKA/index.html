Ex 01 · Compound
Barbell Hip Thrust
P1 · 3×12P2 · 4×10
Chin tucked. Drive through heels. 2s squeeze at the top — load heavier this block.

The most gluteus-maximus-specific loaded hip extension — best single exercise when the priority is the glutes over other muscles.Krause Neto et al., 2025 · Front. Physiol.

Ex 02 · Compound
Romanian Deadlift
P1 · 3×12P2 · 4×10
Hip hinge. Controlled descent. Feel a deep hamstring stretch — push the bottom range.

Loads the glutes and hamstrings at long muscle length — the stretched position is a key driver of lower-limb growth.Pallarés et al., 2021 · Scand. J. Med. Sci. Sports

Ex 03 · Compound
Bulgarian Split Squat
P1 · 3×10P2 · 4×10
Rear foot elevated. Lean forward. Descend to full depth — front knee travels long.

Unilateral glute bias; taken through full range it out-grows partial-range work for glutes and adductors.Kassiano et al., 2023 · J. Strength Cond. Res.

Ex 04 · Compound · NEW
Lateral Step-Up (loaded)
P1 · 3×10P2 · 4×10
Tall box. Drive through the top-foot heel. No push-off from the floor leg.

One of the few moves that loads gluteus maximus very highly (79–113% MVIC) while also hitting gluteus medius — the upper-glute "shelf."Macadam et al., 2015 · Moore et al., 2020 · Int. J. Sports Phys. Ther.

Ex 05 · Accessory
Lying Leg Curl
P1 · 3×12P2 · 4×10
Hips on pad. Slow 2s eccentric. Isolate the hamstrings through knee flexion.

Adds knee-flexion hamstring work the hip-hinge movements miss, balancing the posterior chain.

Ex 06 · Accessory
Cable Hip Abduction
P1 · 3×15P2 · 4×15
Stand tall. Slow 2s eccentric. Focus on the side glute — medius finisher.

Direct gluteus medius isolation for hip shape and the rounded side-glute look.Moore et al., 2020 · Int. J. Sports Phys. Ther.

Ex 07 · Core
Pallof Press
P1 · 3×10P2 · 3×12
Resist the sideways pull. 2s hold at full extension.

Anti-rotation core stability that transfers to every loaded lift.

Ex 01 · Activation · NEW
Scapular Pull-Up
P1 · 3×8P2 · 3×10
Dead hang. Arms straight. Pull shoulder blades down & back — no elbow bend.

Teaches the scapular depression that initiates every pull-up — the missing skill for the first rep.Iversen et al., 2021 · Sports Med.

Ex 02 · Compound · NEW
Banded Pull-Up
P1 · 3×6P2 · 3×8
Full dead-hang start. Chin over bar. Reduce band thickness as you progress.

Assisted pull-up training measurably improves un-assisted pull-up performance in women.Brown, 2019 · UNLV

Ex 03 · Compound · NEW
Negative Pull-Up
P1 · 3×4P2 · 3×5
Jump to the top. Lower for a slow 4–5 seconds under full control.

Eccentric-focused pull-up training raises maximum pulling strength and range.Vigouroux et al., 2024 · Bioengineering

Ex 04 · Compound
Single-Arm DB Row
P1 · 3×10P2 · 4×10
Pull to the hip. Full stretch at the bottom. Fix side-to-side imbalances.

Heavy unilateral rowing builds the back thickness and pulling strength that underpin the pull-up.

Ex 05 · Accessory · NEW
Straight-Arm Cable Pulldown
P1 · 3×12P2 · 3×15
Soft elbows fixed. Drive the bar to the thighs using the lats only.

Isolates the lats in the shoulder-extension pattern used in the pull-up, without the biceps taking over.

Ex 06 · Accessory · NEW
Incline DB Curl
P1 · 3×12P2 · 4×10
Bench at ~45°. Arms hang behind the torso. Full stretch at the bottom, no swing.

Training the biceps in the lengthened position grew the upper-arm biceps more than preacher curls in a trial of 63 women.Kassiano et al., 2024 · Int. J. Sports Med.

Ex 07 · Accessory
Hammer Curl
P1 · 3×12P2 · 4×10
Neutral grip. No swinging. Squeeze at the top.

Targets the brachialis under the biceps — adds arm thickness and grip for the bar.

Ex 08 · Core · NEW
Hanging Knee Raise
P1 · 3×10P2 · 3×12
Dead hang. Curl the knees up, no swing. Control the lower.

Trains the lower abs and builds the grip and dead-hang endurance that carry over directly to pull-ups.

Ex 01 · Compound
Back Squat
P1 · 3×12P2 · 4×10
Depth to at least parallel — deeper if mobility allows. Knees track over toes. Brace hard.

Full-depth squatting built more glute-max thickness than hip thrust in well-trained women — reinstated as a primary.Barbalho et al., 2020 · Int. J. Sports Med. · Plotkin et al., 2023 · Front. Physiol.

Ex 02 · Compound
Walking Lunge
P1 · 3×12P2 · 4×10
Torso upright. Long stride, deep back-knee. Drive through the front heel.

Unilateral quad and glute loading with full-range depth for balanced leg development.

Ex 03 · Accessory
Leg Press
P1 · 3×12P2 · 4×10
Deep controlled descent. Don't lock the knees. Keep the low back on the pad.

High-volume quad overload without spinal fatigue — safe added leg volume.

Ex 04 · Core · NEW
Kneeling Cable Woodchop
P1 · 3×12P2 · 3×15
Tall kneeling. Hips locked and square. Rotate from the mid-back, not the arms.

Kneeling removes the legs so the rotational load goes through the obliques and trunk — direct waist work.

Ex 05 · Core · NEW
Hanging Knee Raise
P1 · 3×10P2 · 3×12
Dead hang. Curl the knees up, no swing. Control the lower.

Lower-ab flexion under load, plus grip and dead-hang endurance that feed the pull-up goal.

Ex 06 · Core
Side Plank Hip Raise
P1 · 3×12P2 · 3×15
Lower the hip to the floor, drive it high. Focus on the bottom-side oblique.

Lateral-chain strength and waist definition through the obliques and QL.

Landmine Press

Ex 03 · Compound · NEW
Inverted Row
P1 · 3×10P2 · 3×12
Body rigid, heels down. Pull the chest to the bar. Lower the bar height to make it harder.

Horizontal bodyweight pulling that builds the same scapular retraction and pulling strength as the pull-up — second weekly pull exposure.Iversen et al., 2021 · Sports Med.

Ex 04 · Accessory
Lateral Raise (cable)
P1 · 4×12P2 · 4×15
Lead with the elbow. Constant tension. Slight lean away from the stack.

Primary side-delt builder for shoulder width and the shoulder-to-waist taper.

Ex 05 · Accessory
Overhead Tricep Extension
P1 · 3×12P2 · 4×10
Elbows in. Lower behind the head for a full stretch. Full extension at the top.

The overhead position grew the triceps ~1.4–1.5× more than neutral-arm work — promoted to the priority triceps move.Maeo et al., 2022 · Eur. J. Sport Sci.

Ex 06 · Accessory · NEW
Cable Tricep Pushdown
P1 · 3×12P2 · 4×12
Upper arms pinned to the sides. Full lockout. Slow return.

Pairs with the overhead extension to train the triceps in both positions — added arm volume toward ~10 sets/week.Schoenfeld et al., 2017 · J. Sports Sci.

Ex 07 · Core
Hollow Body Hold
P1 · 3×25sP2 · 3×35s
Ribs down. Low back pinned to the floor. Legs long.

Isometric whole-core tension that also reinforces the hollow position of the pull-up.

Ex 08 · Core
Ab Wheel Rollout
P1 · 3×8P2 · 3×12
Brace the deep abs. Controlled roll. Keep a rigid plank — no low-back sag.

High-demand anti-extension work for the whole abdominal wall.

HIIT 4×4 — unlocks on cleared bloods
The 4×4 is the right tool for the abdominal-fat goal: interval work at ≥90% max heart rate reduces visceral fat more than steady-state cardio (Zhang et al., 2020), and a 4×4 protocol specifically lowered trunk and visceral fat in women (Sheikholeslami-Vatani et al., 2018).
It stays locked for now because your January panel showed iron-deficiency anaemia (Hb 11.4) and raised inflammation. At that haemoglobin level, VO₂max, peak power and tolerance to maximal-effort intervals are measurably impaired (Miliotis et al., 2025; Pengelly et al., 2024) — so a true 4×4 would be working against the blood chemistry, not with it.
Unlocks when a repeat panel confirms Hb ≥ 12.0 g/dL & CRP < 1.5

Until then — Day 6 default: Zone 2
