Quad & Core
Intensity
RPE 7–8
Ex 01 · Compound
Back Squat
P1 · 3×12 P2 · 3×10 P3 · 4×10
Depth to parallel. Knees track over toes. Brace hard.

Highest-return compound movement for insulin sensitivity and leg strength.

Ex 02 · Compound
Romanian Deadlift
P1 · 3×12 P2 · 3×10 P3 · 4×10
Hip hinge. Controlled descent. Feel the hamstring stretch.

Essential for posterior chain development and glute shape.

Ex 03 · Compound
Walking Lunge
P1 · 3×12 P2 · 3×10 P3 · 4×10
Torso upright. Drive through front heel. Stabilise core.

Unilateral strength and functional stability.

Ex 04 · Accessory
Leg Press
P1 · 3×12 P2 · 3×10 P3 · 4×10
Controlled descent. Don't lock knees. Keep back on pad.

High-volume quad overload without spinal fatigue.

Ex 05 · Accessory
Cable Hip Abduction
P1 · 3×12 P2 · 3×15 P3 · 4×15
Stand tall. Slow 2s eccentric. Focus on the side glute.

Targets glute medius for hip shape and shelf look.

Ex 06a · Core
Dead Bug (weighted)
P1 · 3×8 P2 · 3×10 P3 · 3×12
Lower back flat. Breathe out on reach. Control limbs.

Foundational core stability for heavy lifting.

Ex 06b · Core
Landmine Arc
P1 · 3×8 P2 · 3×10 P3 · 3×12
Rainbow pattern. Hips square. Rotation from mid-back.

Trains anti-rotation and oblique strength.

RPE 7–8
Ex 01 · Compound
Landmine Press
P1 · 3×12 P2 · 3×10 P3 · 4×10
45° arc. Single arm. Full extension.

Safe pressing angle for shoulder health and strength.

Ex 02 · Compound
Push-Up (weighted)
P1 · 3×12 P2 · 3×10 P3 · 4×10
Protract at top. Elbows at 45°. Chest to floor.

Closed-chain pressing for scapular stability.

Ex 03 · Accessory
Lateral Raise (cable)
P1 · 4×12 P2 · 4×12 P3 · 4×15
Lead with elbow. Constant tension. Slight lean.

Primary exercise for shoulder width and V-taper.

Ex 04 · Accessory
Tricep Kickback
P1 · 3×12 P2 · 3×12 P3 · 4×12
Fixed upper arm. Squeeze at top. Slow return.

Isolates the triceps in the shortened position.

Ex 05 · Accessory
Overhead Tricep Extension
P1 · 3×12 P2 · 3×12 P3 · 4×10
Elbows in. Lower behind head. Full extension.

Targets the long head of the tricep for arm shape.

Ex 06 · Core
Hollow Body Hold
P1 · 3×25s P2 · 3×30s P3 · 3×35s
Ribs down. Lower back on floor. Legs long.

Trains isometric core tension and integrated stability.

Ex 07 · Core
Ab Wheel Rollout
P1 · 3×8 P2 · 3×10 P3 · 3×12
Brace TVA. Controlled roll. Rigid plank.

High-demand anti-extension core exercise.

Ex 01 · Compound
Barbell Hip Thrust
P1 · 3×12 P2 · 3×10 P3 · 4×10
Chin tucked. Drive through heels. 1s squeeze.

Single most effective exercise for glute hypertrophy.

Ex 02 · Compound
Bulgarian Split Squat
P1 · 3×10 P2 · 3×10 P3 · 4×10
Rear foot elevated. Lean forward. Control descent.

Unilateral glute bias and hip stability.

Ex 03 · Accessory
Lying Leg Curl
P1 · 3×12 P2 · 3×12 P3 · 4×10
Hips on pad. Slow 2s eccentric. Isolate hamstrings.

Isolates hamstrings through knee flexion.

Ex 04 · Core
Pallof Press
P1 · 3×10 P2 · 3×12 P3 · 3×15
Resist sideways pull. 2s hold at extension.

Ultimate anti-rotation and functional core stability.

Ex 05 · Core
Side Plank Hip Raise
P1 · 3×12 P2 · 3×15 P3 · 3×20
Lower hip to floor. Drive up high. Focus on bottom oblique.

Lateral chain strength and waist definition.

Back & Biceps
Intensity
RPE 7–8
Ex 01 · Compound
Lat Pulldown
P1 · 3×12 P2 · 3×10 P3 · 4×10
Pull to upper chest. Lead with elbows. Squeeze blades.

Primary exercise for back width and V-taper silhouette.

Ex 02 · Compound
Seated Cable Row
P1 · 3×12 P2 · 3×10 P3 · 4×10
Sit tall. Pull to stomach. Squeeze mid-back.

Builds back thickness and improves postural alignment.

Ex 03 · Compound
Single-Arm DB Row
P1 · 3×10 P2 · 3×12 P3 · 4×10
Pull to hip. Feel the stretch. Fix imbalances.

Unilateral back work for balanced strength and growth.

Ex 04 · Accessory
Face Pull
P1 · 3×15 P2 · 3×15 P3 · 4×15
Pull to forehead. Pull rope apart. Focus rear delts.

Essential for shoulder health and rounded posture prevention.

Ex 05 · Accessory
EZ Bar Bicep Curl
P1 · 3×12 P2 · 3×12 P3 · 4×10
Elbows pinned. No swinging. Squeeze at top.

Classic bicep builder for arm shape and peak.

Ex 06 · Accessory
Hammer Curl
P1 · 3×12 P2 · 3×12 P3 · 4×10
Neutral grip. Targets brachialis. Builds arm thickness.

Targets the muscle underneath the bicep for arm height.

Ex 07 · Core
Bird Dog
P1 · 3×10 P2 · 3×12 P3 · 3×15
Flat back. Opposite limbs. 2s hold. Control.

Fundamental core and posterior chain stability.
